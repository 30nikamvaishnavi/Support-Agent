# ShopSphere Store Policies (fictional store, for demo only)

## Returns
Items can be returned within 7 days of delivery. Items must be unused and in the original packaging with tags. Electronics must be returned with all accessories. Personal-care items and innerwear cannot be returned. This is our return policy for all orders.

## Refunds
Approved refunds go back to the original payment method within 5-7 business days after we receive and inspect the item. Cash on delivery refunds are paid to a bank account or as store credit. Refunds above INR 5000 need review by a human agent.

## Shipping and delivery charges
Standard shipping is free on orders of INR 999 or more. Orders below INR 999 pay a shipping fee of INR 49. Standard delivery takes 3-6 business days. Express delivery takes 1-2 business days and costs INR 99 extra in selected cities.

## Delivery delays
If an order is delayed past the estimated date, the new estimated date is shown in the order status. If an order is delayed by more than 7 days, the customer can cancel it for a full refund.

## International shipping
We currently ship only within India. International shipping is not available.

## Warranty
Electronics carry a 12 months manufacturer warranty from the delivery date. The warranty covers manufacturing defects only, not physical damage or water damage. Warranty claims need the order ID and proof of purchase.

## Payment methods
We accept UPI, credit cards, debit cards and net banking. Cash on delivery is available for orders up to INR 5000.

## Order cancellation
Orders can be cancelled free of charge while the status is Processing. Once an order is Shipped it cannot be cancelled, but it can be returned after delivery under the return policy.

## Changing the delivery address
The delivery address can be changed only while the order status is Processing. Contact support with the order ID to change it.

## Contact and escalation
Complex issues, refunds above INR 5000 and complaints that need review are handed to a human agent. A human agent contacts the customer within 24 hours.
